
# Adidas Sales Data Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Adidas sales data to evaluate product performance, revenue, operating profit, regional sales distribution, retailer contributions, and sales channel performance.

The objective is to turn sales data into actionable business insights that can support better inventory planning, pricing, marketing, and sales decisions.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Data cleaning and preparation
- PivotTables
- Data visualization and dashboard reporting

## Dataset
The workbook contains 9,648 sales records with fields including:
- Retailer and product category
- Invoice date
- Region, state, and city
- Price per unit and units sold
- Total sales and operating profit
- Operating margin
- Sales method

## Key Business Questions
1. Which product categories generate the highest sales?
2. Which regions contribute the most revenue?
3. How do sales and profitability vary by retailer?
4. How do In-store, Outlet, and Online channels compare?
5. What sales trends can support inventory and marketing decisions?

## Key Findings
The analysis identified Men's Street Footwear as a leading revenue-generating product category. The West region recorded the highest total sales in the PivotTable analysis.

The report also examined retailer contributions, sales channel performance, and seasonal trends to identify potential areas for business improvement.

## Recommendations
- Prioritize inventory for high-performing product categories.
- Align inventory planning with seasonal demand.
- Evaluate sales channel profitability before allocating marketing resources.
- Review retailer performance and regional sales trends.
- Use data-driven insights to support pricing and promotional decisions.

## Project Files
- `Adidas_Sales_Analysis.xlsx` — Excel workbook containing the dataset, cleaned data, PivotTable analysis, and dashboard.
- `Adidas_Sales_Analysis_Report.pdf` — Written report containing findings and strategic recommendations.
